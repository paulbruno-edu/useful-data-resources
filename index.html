    About this list

    What's included. Sources whose data are free to download or free to request, plus a few that need an application (marked "Application or restricted"). Descriptions in quotation marks are from the publisher.

    How it's kept current. Every link is checked automatically, and moved or broken links are fixed by hand. Each entry also has an "archived" link to the Internet Archive's most recent copy, in case a page moves or goes offline.

    Caveats. Access rules and units of analysis were recorded from each source's main page and can change; check the source's documentation before you plan around them.
